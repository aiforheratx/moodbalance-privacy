Atara Support

If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, please don't wait for an email. In the US, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or text HOME to 741741 (Crisis Text Line). Elsewhere, contact your local emergency number. Atara is a wellness app and cannot provide crisis care.

Common questions

Where is my data? Everything you log stays on your iPhone in an encrypted database. There are no accounts and no servers. See the privacy policy.

How do I back up or move my data? Atara's data is included in your normal iPhone backup (iCloud or computer). Restoring your backup on a new phone restores Atara's data. You can also export everything as JSON from Profile → Data Export.

Why do insights need a few days of data? Atara looks for patterns in your history, so it needs some mood logs and Health data before associations are statistically meaningful. Keep logging and connect Apple Health for the fastest start.

Why is the AI reflection unavailable on my phone? The AI-phrased insights use Apple's on-device intelligence, which requires a compatible iPhone with Apple Intelligence enabled. Without it, Atara still works fully — insights use built-in phrasing instead.

How do I connect my ring or wearable? Any ring or watch that syncs to Apple Health (Oura, RingConn, Ultrahuman, Apple Watch, …) works automatically once you grant Health access. See Settings → Rings & Wearables.

How do I delete my data? Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your device. Health entries Atara saved to Apple Health can be managed in the Health app.
