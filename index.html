Atara Privacy Policy

Atara is the app formerly developed as “MoodBalance”.

Effective date: July 24, 2026

Atara is built on a simple principle: your health data belongs to you and stays on your device. The app has no backend, no analytics, no advertising, and no account system that stores your data in the cloud.

What data the app handles

Atara works with the following information, all of which you provide or authorize:

  • Mood entries and journal notes you log in the app
  • Apple Health (HealthKit) data you grant access to — such as heart rate variability, sleep, activity, menstrual cycle, biomarkers, and body measurements
  • Medications, supplements, and health conditions you record
  • Profile details you optionally share (such as age, working style, or family context) to personalize insights
  • Work and life-context entries you log

Where your data lives

All of this data is stored only on your device, in an encrypted local database protected by iOS file protection (it is unreadable while your device is locked). It is included in your device backups according to your own iOS backup settings, which you control.

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data. We cannot see it. It never reaches our servers, because there are no servers.

On-device intelligence

All analysis in Atara — pattern detection, mood forecasting, cycle prediction, and AI-phrased insights — runs entirely on your device using Apple's on-device foundation models. No prompt, insight, or health detail is ever sent to a cloud AI service.

Apple Health

Atara reads Health data only with your explicit permission, which you can change at any time in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security → Health. With your permission, entries you log (such as period or body-measurement records) can also be saved back to Apple Health. Health data is used solely to generate your personal insights on your device and is never used for advertising or shared with third parties.

Optional wearable connections

If you choose to connect a wearable ring through Apple Health, its data flows through HealthKit under the same rules above. If you explicitly connect an Oura account, the app communicates directly between your device and Oura's API to fetch your own data; that data is then stored only on your device. This connection is entirely optional and off by default.

Sign in with Apple

If you use Sign in with Apple, it is used only to identify you on your own device. No identifier is transmitted to us.

Notifications

Medication and logging reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification content passes through external servers.

Exports you create

Features such as the clinician PDF report create files at your request. You decide whether and with whom to share them; the app never sends them anywhere on its own.

Children's privacy

Atara is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly handle their data.

Not medical advice

Atara is a wellness companion. It does not provide medical diagnoses or treatment and is not a substitute for professional medical or mental-health care.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new effective date. Because the app collects nothing, changes would only ever describe new on-device features.
